## About me

Biostatistics leader with experience in vaccine clinical development across Phase 1 to Phase 4 and post-marketing studies. Expertise includes statistical strategy, study design, protocol and SAP development, interim and final analyses, regulatory submissions, and cross-functional leadership.

My work has covered both prophylactic and therapeutic vaccine programs, with experience in translational research and global development settings. I am also interested in R/Shiny tool development, simulation, Bayesian methods, missing data methods, and practical statistical tools for decision-making.
